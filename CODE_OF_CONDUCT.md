# Code of conduct

vx follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated, in any project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability") or by opening a confidential discussion with a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.
